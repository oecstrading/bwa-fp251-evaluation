Run the evidence trail

  1. Ingest the public FP251 records. Check that the published receipt remains separate from approved financing and that actual expenditure is pending reconciliation.
  2. Sync the sample feeds, run quality checks and simulate reviewer decisions. Review the finance variance and the meter and laboratory exceptions.
  3. Open the report preview. Download financing, results and milestone registers, reconciliation and exception schedules, and sample audit events.

Project registration and documentary links are in GCFT. Accounting, schedule, sensor and lab feeds in this browser exercise are synthetic. Official reporting depends on BWA feeds, approved definitions and authorized review.

Measure reporting effort

The illustrative case reduces preparation from 64 to 24 staff-hours per reporting cycle. At 12 cycles, that represents 480 staff-hours per year. During acceptance, BWA staff would time the same pack before and after, verify completeness and confirm actual operating costs.
